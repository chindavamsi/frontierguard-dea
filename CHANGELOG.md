The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),

No user-facing changes yet.

## [0.1.0a1] - 2026-08-07

### Added

- Typed data containers and immutable, auditable result objects.
- Input- and output-oriented radial envelopment models.
- CRS, VRS, NIRS, and NDRS technology options.
- Optional second-phase slack maximization, targets, and peers.
- Separate evaluation and reference technologies.
- Weighted additive, directional-distance, and non-oriented SBM models.
- Exact input- and output-oriented Free Disposal Hull models.
- Radial super-efficiency, scale efficiency, and RTS diagnosis.
- CRS multiplier DEA, selected cross-efficiency appraisal, and type-I
  assurance-region restrictions.
- Cost and revenue efficiency with Farrell technical/allocative decomposition,
  common or per-DMU prices, and price provenance.
- Adjacent-period CRS/VRS Malmquist productivity for aligned panels, with four
  distance terms and audited efficiency/technical-change decomposition.
- CRS relational serial-network DEA with stage-efficiency decomposition.
- Experimental graph-defined relational DEA for arbitrary acyclic networks.
- Native HiGHS and SciPy-HiGHS adapters with independently audited solutions.
- Machine-readable method registry, command-line diagnostics, and
  reproducibility evidence bundles.
- Mathematical contracts, ecosystem matrix, validation policy, and roadmap.
- Community governance, security, contribution, citation, and release
  automation.
- Versioned scholarly metadata and contributor-role records.
- FrontierGuard DEA White Paper v0.1 as a non-peer-reviewed technical report.
- An executable conformance-benchmark protocol with machine-readable cases,
  result contracts, and adapter specifications for independent implementations.
